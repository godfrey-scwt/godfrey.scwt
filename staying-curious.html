Staying curious about what I don't yet speak

There's something refreshing about being curious enough to understand more than one language. The words change, the grammar bends in unexpected ways, and yet underneath it all is the same humanity: the same jokes, worries, and hopes, just dressed differently. Every new language reminds me that the distance between people is smaller than it looks.

Coding gives me the same feeling. Python, Excel, and SQL each have their own syntax and their own way of thinking. Alone, each is useful. Together, they're not competing; they're telling one story, with the data moving from one language to the next without losing its meaning.

In both worlds, the real skill is translation.

Spoken languages carry meaning between people, and coding languages carry it between ideas and systems. Either way, each new language makes you a better listener, a better builder, and a little more connected to everyone and everything else.
